Zimbabwe Company Registration

Transform your business ideas into profit. With our expertise, we meticulously assess your startup needs, ensuring a cost-effective business launch. Whether you’re looking to register a Private Limited Company or a Private Business Corporation, we’re here to help.

Learn more

Turn your business dreams into reality by registering a Private Limited Company (PLC) or a Private Business Corporation (PBC). Both are governed by the Companies Act but differ in documentation.

A PLC is a popular choice in Zimbabwe, offering limited liability and comprehensive documentation, ideal for simplicity and protection. PBCs, on the other hand, are favored by professionals like web developers, lawyers, and consultants, especially post-pandemic.

We not only assist with business registration but also provide startup advice and integrated tax and accounting services, ensuring a smooth, cost-effective launch and ongoing growth.
