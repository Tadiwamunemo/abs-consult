Non-resident shareholders tax (NRST) treatment in Zimbabwe.

What is non-resident shareholders tax?

Non-resident shareholders tax is tax levied on a dividend accruing to a person who is not resident in Zimbabwe.

Who is a non-resident?

A non-resident means a person, other than a company, who is not ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe, or a partnership or foreign company which is not ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe. The definition is met any time a resident taxpayer in Zimbabwe makes a payment to a foreign resident entity or individual.

For NRST, a dividend is income distributed by a company to its members as a return on capital they have invested. Every company that distributes a dividend is required to withhold the tax when it is paid to:

  • A person, other than a company, a pension fund, a benefit fund, or a medical aid society, who is not ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe;
  • A partnership which is not ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe;
  • A foreign company; or
  • A foreign life insurance company, in respect of any shareholding the Commissioner determines was acquired from funds other than those arising from that company’s life insurance business in Zimbabwe.

Non-resident shareholders tax is levied at 15% of the gross dividend paid by an unlisted company, and at 10% where the paying company is listed. A lower rate can apply where a double taxation agreement is in place between Zimbabwe and the non-resident’s country of residence.

Distributions that are not dividends for this tax

  • Certain amounts distributed by a building society that are not distributed as a dividend on the specified classes of shares for the Central Africa Building Society, the Founders Building Society, and the Beverley Building Society.
  • Bonus shares.
  • Any amount which, in the opinion of the Commissioner, is a return of the amount received by the company for its shares.
  • Amounts distributed by the Industrial Development Corporation of Zimbabwe Limited in respect of its issued share capital.
  • Amounts distributed by the Zimbabwe Development Bank, established by section 3 of the Zimbabwe Development Bank Act [Chapter 24:14].
  • Amounts distributed to the International Finance Corporation referred to in the International Financial Organizations Act [Chapter 22:09].
  • Amounts distributed by a licensed investor which arise from operations in an export processing zone.
  • Any amount which, in the opinion of the Commissioner, is a return of an amount contributed to the capital of a private business corporation by a member.
  • Amounts distributed by an industrial park developer which arise from the operation of that industrial park.

When the dividend is deemed distributed

A dividend is deemed to have been distributed when it is paid to the shareholder, credited to the shareholder’s account, or otherwise dealt with so that the shareholder becomes entitled to it — whichever comes first.

The withholding certificate

Once NRST is withheld, the payer must give the shareholder a withholding tax certificate showing the gross amount of the dividend, any reduction of the dividend for the purpose of computing NRST, and the amount of non-resident shareholders tax withheld.
